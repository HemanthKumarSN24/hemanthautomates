Workflow Build Sprint

$1,500–6,000 · delivered in a 5-day sprint

One manual process, fully automated end to end in five days: from intake, through AI processing, through a human approval checkpoint, to a verified write into your system of record — with an audit trail for every decision the system made.

The pattern I build

  • Intake — email, form, webhook or file drop: whatever way work enters your business today
  • AI processing — classification, extraction, routing, drafting. LLM APIs with structured output and confidence scoring
  • Confidence gate — the system knows what it doesn't know. Low-confidence cases route to a human, never guess
  • Human approval checkpoint — a simple review queue where a person approves or corrects before anything touches your records
  • System write — approved outcomes written to your CRM, database, or documents, with an immutable audit trail

What "done" means

A working system processing your real workload — not a proof of concept. You get: the live workflow, a walkthrough session so your team can run it, documentation of every decision point, and the audit trail showing exactly what was automated, when, and who approved what.

Built with

n8n orchestration, LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, or open models), Python where logic demands it, PostgreSQL-backed state for anything that must not lose a transaction. Every build ships with a supervised automation demo pattern you can see running before you commit.

Why a sprint, not a project

Month-long automation projects die in scoping. Five days forces the discipline that matters: ship the highest-ROI path first, measure it live, and iterate. Clients who need more runs a second sprint on the next process — most do.
