What this demo is. Every message on the left arrived from a resident (sample data). On arrival the agent reads it, classifies it, sets a priority and SLA target from the site's rules, drafts the resident reply, and proposes a staff assignment. Staff only see a queue of confirmed decisions — zero requests sit unread, and breaches surface before they happen instead of at month-end.

In production this connects to Fresh's WhatsApp Business number and email, and writes into your job sheet / CRM. The demo runs entirely on sample data in this browser.
