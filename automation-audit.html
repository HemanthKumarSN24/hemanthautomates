AI Automation Audit

$200–300 · delivered in 1 week

Every business runs on manual work nobody counts: copying data between tools, checking documents by hand, chasing approvals, re-typing the same email. The audit answers one question with numbers — which of your processes should be automated first, and what is each one actually costing you?

What you get

  • Workflow map — your top 5 manual processes documented step by step, including the invisible ones (that spreadsheet only one person knows how to use)
  • Time-and-cost quantification — hours spent per week per process, translated into monthly cost at your team's rates
  • Automation roadmap — each process scored on ROI: what to automate first, what to leave alone, what AI can safely handle vs what needs human approval
  • Build-or-buy verdict — for each candidate: use an off-the-shelf tool, or is a custom agentic workflow worth building

How it works

Day 1: 45-minute call — I ask about where work piles up, what gets done twice, what errors cost you.

Days 2–4: I shadow the workflows (screen-share walkthroughs, not questionnaires), document each process, and measure.

Day 5: You receive the roadmap — a short document with the numbers, the priorities, and a one-page build plan for the #1 candidate. If you want, I build it — most audit clients do.

Who it's for

Small teams (2–30 people) drowning in operational overhead: property management, logistics, retail back-office, professional services, healthcare admin. If your team's answer to "how do we keep on top of this" is "someone checks it manually," this audit is for you.

Why an audit first, not a build?

Because most automation money is wasted on the wrong process. The obvious candidate is rarely the highest-ROI one — and building before measuring means automating a workflow that shouldn't exist at all. The audit pays for itself by telling you not to spend money where you were about to.
